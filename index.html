How we work

Not an agency.
An owner–operator.

We don’t take briefs or bill hours. We hold the code, the brand, the store listings and the customer relationship — so every improvement we make compounds inside our own portfolio, not someone else’s.

  1. 01

    Spot

    We find durable, everyday needs that existing apps serve poorly — and validate demand before we write a line.

  2. 02

    Build

    A small in-house team designs and engineers every app natively, on a shared foundation that makes each launch faster.

  3. 03

    Publish

    We ship under our own developer accounts and own distribution end to end — store presence, growth and support.

  4. 04

    Compound

    Subscriptions and in-app revenue fund the next product. Learnings, code and audience carry across the whole portfolio.
